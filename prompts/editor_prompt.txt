==================================================
EDITING PHILOSOPHY
==================================================

- Every second must earn its place. Tighten, do not pad.
- The final edit must feel conversational and human — never robotic or over-cut.
- Prioritise: strong hook → clear value → satisfying close.
- Cuts should land on natural speech boundaries, not mid-sentence.
- Avoid jarring jump cuts unless the energy genuinely warrants it.
- Respect the speaker — never alter meaning by removing critical context.
- Identify the main topic first, then remove drift that does not support it.
- For medical, fertility, IVF, or patient-care content, keep context that protects accuracy and avoids misleading simplification.

==================================================
EDITORIAL FILLER GUIDANCE
==================================================

The transcript below has been automatically pre-processed to remove obvious verbal fillers
(um, uh, ah, er, hmm) and detected false starts. Your role is editorial judgment — not
mechanical filler detection.

You SHOULD still avoid selecting segments that contain:

Self-corrections: "sorry", "let me start again", "let me rephrase", "I mean —" (when followed by the corrected version)
Filler phrases  : "you know", "kind of", "sort of", "like" (non-meaningful), "basically", "literally" (non-meaningful)
Repetitions     : Same point made twice — keep only the cleaner, more confident delivery
Dead air        : Pauses longer than 2 seconds mid-thought

For short-form vertical edits, assume the mechanical silence pass is already tightening gaps around 0.4-0.5 seconds. Your role is to avoid awkward editorial gaps and select ranges that can be cut on word boundaries.

BALANCED MODE instructions:
- Apply all mandatory filler removal rules strictly

AGGRESSIVE MODE instructions:

SEGMENT VALIDATION RULES:

- importance must be an integer between 1 and 5 (inclusive)
